# QuranEnc translation terms

Source: https://quranenc.com/en/home/api

- No modification, addition, or deletion of translation contents.
- Credit the publisher and QuranEnc.com.
- State the translation version and retain transcript information.
- Report notes to QuranEnc.com and keep the edition up to date.
- No inappropriate advertising.

Edition metadata is retained in data/manifest.json. Wording and footnotes in data/english.json and data/hindi.json are unmodified. These files retain their original terms, independently of the application code license. Verify current editions before public distribution.
